# SnardgeUtils

WoW Forever addon with small quality of life tools: Bagnon item marks for selling, banking and mailing, xp/hr and pending quest XP on the experience bar, a self-updating target macro for nearby quest mobs, a low health warning sound, LFG filters and more.

The addon lives in [`SnardgeUtils/`](SnardgeUtils/), see its [README](SnardgeUtils/README.md) for features, installation and commands.
